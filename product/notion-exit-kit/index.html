The problem

You pay $10–$20 per seat every month for Notion. You press Export, you get a zip — and your workspace doesn't come with it. Databases come out as flat CSVs of one view. Relations become plain-text titles. Formulas become frozen numbers. Your board, calendar and timeline views don't export at all. Comments aren't in the Markdown export. And users who paid for a full year have reported being locked out of their own workspaces when they tried to downgrade. A 10-person team on Business ($20/seat/month billed yearly) pays at least $2,400 a year.

What it does

  • Audits your export — reads the real Notion export zip (including the zip-inside-a-zip shape, Export-<id>-Part-1.zip) and inventories pages, databases, rows and relations.
  • Rebuilds a clean vault — strips the 32-character Notion ID from every filename and rewrites internal links, so the pages open directly in Obsidian or any Markdown tool.
  • Rebuilds your databases in SQLite — one table per database, plus a `pages` table and a `relations` table that links database rows back to the pages they pointed at.
  • Tells you what is lost, honestly — a loss report lists views, formula logic, comments and permissions as gone for good, and flags every relation it could not resolve, so nothing fails silently.

What’s inside

  • SKILL.md — the full step-by-step skill: export, audit, rebuild relations, rebuild views, save formulas, cancel, verify.
  • scripts/notion_exit.py — the audit/rebuild script (stdlib only, tested with --selftest). No API key, no account access, no network.

Worked example — real output

A small test export — 2 pages (Projects, Design) and a Tasks database with 2 rows whose Project column points at the Design page. Real output: Pages: 2, Databases: 1, Rows: 2, Relations resolved: 2, Unresolved: 0. Vault: out/vault. SQLite: out/notion_exit.sqlite. Report: out/loss-report.md. The vault holds clean files — Projects.md links to Design.md, not to 'Design bbbb….md'. The SQLite database holds the tasks table and a relations table with both rows linked back to the Design page. The loss report reads: what survived — pages 2 (Markdown, links rewritten to clean names), databases 1 with 2 rows now in notion_exit.sqlite, relations resolved back to pages 2. What is lost for good — views (board/calendar/gallery/timeline layouts are rendering instructions, not stored data; only the rows survive), formula and rollup logic (the CSV holds the value the formula computed at export time, not the formula), comments (not in a Markdown & CSV export at all — only in the HTML export), permissions and sharing (a Notion-side setting, does not export).

This is the output of the included script on the stated inputs, not a promise for your setup. Your numbers will differ; the skill prices yours.

Honest limits

  • It works from the export file. It cannot recover what Notion did not export: views, formula logic, comments (unless you ran the HTML export), permissions, synced-block links.
  • It cannot bypass an export disabled by a workspace owner, and it cannot recover other people's private pages.
  • It does not use the Notion API — there is no export endpoint, and walking the block tree is throttled at about 3 requests per second.
  • If a database shows fewer rows than Notion showed, the export was filtered by the view you exported. Switch the current view to an unfiltered table and re-export.
